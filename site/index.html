Durable Concrete Blocks & Paving Stones Manufactured in Kampala

Graded stone, sand and cement are moved by loader into the batching bays. What goes in decides what comes out, so the inputs are selected before anything is mixed.

Batched by weight, not by guess

Consistent proportioning is what makes one block behave like the next. Our crews work the hopper in full protective equipment, every batch to the same recipe.

Into the plant

Mixed concrete is conveyed to the moulding machine, where vibration and compression give each unit its density. This is where a block stops being a mixture and becomes a component.

Continuous production

The plant runs to order. Freshly moulded units come off the line and move straight to the curing field, which is how we hold bulk quantities without holding up your site.

Curing is not waiting

Units are laid out and cured before they are stacked or loaded. Rushing this stage is the single most common reason a block fails on site. We do not rush it.

Solid block

Maximum density where the load demands it — foundations, retaining courses, high-load walling and anywhere a hollow unit would be the weak link.

Pavers

Interlocking units in several profiles and colours. They spread load across the surface instead of through it, which is why a paved driveway outlasts a poured one under vehicle traffic.

  • UseDriveways & walkways
  • ProfilesMultiple
  • ColoursGrey & red

Kerb stones

The edge that holds everything else in place. Without restraint, paving creeps and gravel migrates — kerbs are what keep a finished surface looking finished in year five.

  • UseRoads & landscaping
  • FormPrecast kerb
  • FinishCast edge
